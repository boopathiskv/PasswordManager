🔐 Security

  • 🛡️ AES-GCM 256-bit authenticated encryption
  • 🔑 PBKDF2 600,000 iterations + random salt
  • 🔄 XOR layer with SHA-256 derived key
  • 📝 Base64 encoded payload
  • 🔢 6-digit PIN protection per record
  • ⏱️ Auto-hide passwords after 8 seconds
  • 📋 Clipboard auto-clears after 20 seconds
  • 🛡️ Strict Content-Security-Policy — no inline scripts, no third-party CDNs
